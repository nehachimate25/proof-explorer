Proof Explorer

Enter a statement you want to prove. The backend finds out which proof methods fit it, then walks through the proof one step at a time. If a method does not fit, it tells you why instead of forcing a proof.

Write it as a sentence, for example “If n is even, then n² is even.”

Or start from an example

How a proof is checked

  1. Your statement is read and its logical form is found, such as P → Q.
  2. The backend decides which methods suit that form: direct proof, contrapositive, contradiction or induction.
  3. You choose a suitable method and step through the proof. Each step names the reason that makes it valid.
  4. A proof is only labelled Verified when the backend has actually checked it.
